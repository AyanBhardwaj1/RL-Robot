Method

  • Score = 45% margin index (push envelope 40%, other stressors 60%) + 40% nominal pass rate under domain-randomised conditions + 15% adversarial (penalises failure modes found at severity ≤ 0.5).
  • Pass = no fall (tilt > 65° or body clearance < 11 cm) and ≥ 35% of commanded distance covered.
  • Failure-rate bound: exact binomial with zero failures (≈3/n), Wilson score otherwise.
